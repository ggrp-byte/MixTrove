Your YouTube Mixes, collected.

MixTrove copies the tracks from the Mix you are listening to into a private playlist on your own YouTube account — duplicate-free, by hand or automatically.

Get MixTrove Privacy policy

What it does

  • Saves the current Mix queue to a playlist you choose, skipping tracks you already have.
  • Adds the MixTrove panel to YouTube Mix pages, so you can pick or create the playlist without leaving the page.
  • With Pro, collects new tracks in the background while you listen.
  • Works in 25 languages and follows the YouTube light and dark theme.

MixTrove is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google. Your archive is a normal private YouTube playlist; MixTrove does not download audio.

Support

MixTrove is a small independent project. If something does not work as described, or you have a question, get in touch — every report is read.

  • Report a problem or ask a question: open an issue at github.com/ggrp-byte/MixTrove/issues. Please include the extension version, your browser and what you expected to happen.
  • Pro billing: subscriptions are processed by ExtensionPay. Use the Billing button in the MixTrove popup to review, change or cancel a subscription.
  • Privacy: see the privacy policy for exactly what MixTrove stores and what it never collects.
